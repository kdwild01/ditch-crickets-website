A Relentless Offense & Chaos on the Basepaths

The 12U Friendswood Ditch Crickets wrapped up an exciting, fast-paced Fall 2026 campaign defined by explosive innings, aggressive baserunning, and relentless plate discipline. Across 10 fall games and scrimmages, the Crickets averaged over 9 runs per contest, piling up 79 hits—including 22 doubles, a triple, and 4 home runs—while drawing 52 walks to post a massive .517 team on-base percentage.

Once aboard, the Ditch Crickets turned up the pressure, swiping 63 bases at a 95.5% success rate. Leading the charge on the basepaths were Hank Huter (11-for-11 in steals, 16 runs scored), Jarrett Dillard (10-for-10, 9 walks), Devyn Nguyen (.600 AVG, .733 OBP, 8 steals), Lincoln Elam (.458 AVG, 11 RBIs, 8 steals), and Grant Towler (7-for-7 in steals).

Top Moments From the Fall Slate

  • Opening Statement (Oct. 24): The Crickets kicked off tournament play by exploding for 6 runs in the first inning—fueled by RBI doubles from Jarrett Dillard, Simon Kissell, and Devyn Nguyen—and hung on behind strong mound work from Colin Wild and Cason Cox to edge Black Sox Nolan, 7–6.
  • Power Surge & Scrimmage Fireworks (Nov. 1 – Nov. 12): The bats caught fire during the November slate. Colin Wild drove in 5 runs in a 10-run third inning to power a 15–6 win over the Houston Bengals. One week later, Lincoln Elam crushed a two-run homer to right field while Grant Towler and Colin Wild each collected 3 hits in a 13–10 victory over the Space City Trash Pandas, followed by a 10–8 scrimmage victory over the Bayside Bananas sparked by a 5-run third-inning rally.
  • Long Balls & Heartbreakers (Oct. 25 & Nov. 21): Grant Towler went a perfect 3-for-3 with a center-field blast against SoCo Show VI and added another moonshot to center against Bayside Bananas later in the month. In one of the wildest games of the fall, Gaige Atessis delivered a clutch 3-run double after a 7-pitch battle to put the Crickets up 13–8 against STX Coyotes before falling in a 14–13 walk-off thriller.
  • Dominant Finale Win (Nov. 22): The Crickets closed out their tournament weekend in style with a 14–4 rout of the Waller County Underdawgs. Gaige Atessis went 3-for-3 with a solo home run, Hank Huter added 3 hits and 2 RBIs, pickup standout Samuel Beran drove in 4 runs, and Lincoln Elam struck out 4 over three innings of work without allowing an earned run.

Thank You to Our Players, Families & Pickup Crew

From turning 6 double plays in the field to battling through every inning, our core roster—alongside fall pickup contributors Samuel Beran (.600 AVG, 4 doubles), Kaiden Lukens (.750 AVG, 4 RBIs), and Nicholas Lewin—showed tremendous growth all season long. A huge thank you to our parents, families, Manager Jonathon Dillard, and Coaches Kevin Wild, Matt Hermes, and Kyle Kissell for making Fall 2026 a memorable run on the dirt.
